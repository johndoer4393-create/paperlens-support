Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

PaperLens is a document scanning application designed to help users scan, organize, read, save, search, and share documents.

Information We Collect

PaperLens does not require users to create an account or sign in.

PaperLens does not sell users' personal information.

Camera Access

PaperLens requests access to the device camera so users can scan documents. Camera access is used only to provide document-scanning functionality.

Scanned Documents

Documents scanned with PaperLens are processed for features such as cropping, rotation, image enhancement, PDF creation, text recognition (OCR), document search, saving, and sharing.

Documents saved within PaperLens are stored on the user's device. Users control when documents are saved, shared, exported, or deleted.

Text Recognition (OCR)

PaperLens can recognize text contained in scanned documents. Recognized text may be used within the app to display extracted text and help users search their saved documents.

Sharing

PaperLens allows users to share documents using sharing options available on their device. PaperLens does not control the privacy practices of third-party applications or services selected by the user for sharing.

Children's Privacy

PaperLens does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as PaperLens develops. Any changes will be posted on this page.
